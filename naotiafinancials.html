Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026

Naotia Financials ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in privacy-first architecture.

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or process any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location).
  • No Server Storage: All financial calculations, inputs (such as Asset Prices, Strikes, and Volatilities), and user-built strategies are processed locally on your device's processor.

2. Local Data Storage

The App utilizes local device storage solely to save your Calculation History. This allows you to review past pricing scenarios and multi-leg strategies locally. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third-party servers. You can clear your history at any time directly within the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data-broker SDKs.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App is designed for financial professionals, traders, and students. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
